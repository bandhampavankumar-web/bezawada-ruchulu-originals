Our Roots

Bezawada Ruchulu Originals

Originating from the culinary hub of Vijayawada (Bezawada), our brand brings you original flavors crafted in a traditional, authentic, clean kitchen environment.

Every single batch of our Karapodi, Karam, Sweets, and Delicacies is prepared with strict supervision, using top-tier grains, pulses, and locally farmed condiments. We grind and roast our ingredients in classic heritage ratios so you enjoy the exact home-made taste of traditional Andhra meals. No added coloring, no chemicals, just pure goodness.
